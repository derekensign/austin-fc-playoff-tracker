How the Shield is decided

What counts. 3 points for a win, 1 for a draw, 0 for a loss, on the 90-minute result of every completed league match. Cup and playoff matches excluded. The official Copa Tejas competition awards head-to-head trophies within MLS and within the USL Championship, and a Shield across every division and gender on average points per game; this page computes that Shield ranking from the results feed instead of by hand, and adds USL League One clubs the official table omits.

Tiebreakers. points per game, then goal difference per game, then goals for per game, then club name. Clubs in different leagues never meet, so the head-to-head, disciplinary and coin-toss tiebreakers the official competition uses within a league cannot apply across leagues; per-game goal figures stand in for them.

Season boundaries. A club’s record is its current league season as the results feed labels it. When a league’s new season begins, its clubs restart from zero, so a club can lead on two matches while another has played thirty. Nothing is carried over or pro-rated.

Who is included. Every professional club based in Texas: Austin FC, FC Dallas and Houston Dynamo FC (MLS); Houston Dash (NWSL); El Paso Locomotive FC, San Antonio FC and Rio Grande Valley FC Toros (USL Championship); Dallas Trinity FC (USL Super League); Corpus Christi FC and Texoma FC (USL League One). Austin FC II, Houston Dynamo FC 2 and North Texas SC (MLS Next Pro) appear as reserve sides only. A club is ranked once it has completed a league match this season; until then it is listed as not ranked rather than dropped silently.

Sources

Results are pulled hourly from the American Soccer Analysis feed for every league, and every club’s record is rebuilt from its full season game log each run. The page shows its data date, the latest completed match involving a Texas club, rather than claiming to be live.
